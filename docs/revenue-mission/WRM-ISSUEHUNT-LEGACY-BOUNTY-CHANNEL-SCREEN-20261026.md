# WRM IssueHunt legacy bounty channel screen — 2026-10-26

## Decision

**CLOSED_PENDING_REACTIVATION — NO BUILD, CLAIM, ACCOUNT CREATION, OR OUTREACH**

IssueHunt is a historically legitimate issue-bounty channel, but the public evidence available on 2026-10-26 does not establish a current, claimable, AI-compatible task with credible payout. The channel is therefore removed from the active commercial frontier until fresh evidence satisfies the re-entry conditions below.

This is a reversible channel screen, not a claim that IssueHunt is fraudulent or permanently inactive.

## Commercial predicate tested

Can Waterfound select an IssueHunt task that is simultaneously:

1. currently open and technically unresolved;
2. funded and plausibly payable now;
3. not already saturated or claimed;
4. compatible with AI-assisted or agent-authored work;
5. admissible without new paid spend, outreach, account installation, KYC, contract acceptance, or public publication; and
6. valuable enough to justify a narrow proof before any human gate?

**Result: no.**

## Evidence

### Capability / channel history

- IssueHunt's public repository describes an issue-based bounty platform: <https://github.com/IssueHunt/readme>
- A historical IssueHunt bot record on BoostNote issue 2631 says that a sponsor funded **$10.00** on 2019-04-22 and links contributors to IssueHunt: <https://github.com/BoostIO/BoostNote-Legacy/issues/2631>

This establishes historical channel capability only. It does not establish current task availability, current payout assurance, or current contributor eligibility.

### Current observed outcomes

- On 2026-09-08, a prospective contributor opened IssueHunt/readme issue 12 because many catalog entries pointed to GitHub issues closed years earlier and sorting appeared unreliable. They explicitly asked whether OSS bounties were still claimable in 2026: <https://github.com/IssueHunt/readme/issues/12>
- As observed on 2026-10-26, that question remained open, unassigned, unlabeled, and unanswered.
- The concrete funded example inspected, BoostNote issue 2631, was opened in 2018, funded in 2019, and resides in a repository archived read-only on 2026-05-12.
- The issue thread also records in July 2019 that the affected behavior no longer occurred in the then-current BoostNote release.
- The public IssueHunt terms route required JavaScript and did not expose enough text to verify current AI-assistance, automation, payout, or submission rules in this read-only screen: <https://issuehunt.io/terms>

### Competitive / substitution finding

A stale bounty catalog creates more validation and settlement risk than direct, current task sources. It cannot presently beat channels that expose all of the following at selection time: a live maintainer, current issue state, explicit reward amount, supported submission path, and clear AI-assistance policy.

### Demand and willingness to pay

- Historical funding observed: **$10**, posted in 2019.
- Current willingness to pay: **not established**.
- Current payout/escrow assurance: **not established**.
- Customer demand: **not established**.
- Revenue realized: **$0**.

No ROI, operator-hour savings, approval reduction, or monetary-risk reduction is claimed.

## Kill criteria applied

The channel fails the current selection test because:

1. the platform has not publicly answered its own current claimability question;
2. the inspected funded task is attached to an archived repository and an apparently obsolete/resolved defect;
3. no current AI/agent contribution policy was verified;
4. no current payout assurance was verified; and
5. no unsaturated, technically live task with positive expected value was found.

Adding runtime, telemetry, or a speculative implementation would not answer these missing commercial predicates cheaply. The correct action is to stop.

## Re-entry conditions

Reopen IssueHunt screening only if public, current evidence supplies all of:

1. a task whose upstream issue is open, maintained, reproducible, and not already solved;
2. a reward with a current amount and documented funding or settlement mechanism;
3. an explicit supported submission and claim path;
4. explicit permission for AI-assisted or agent-authored contributions, or written task-specific acceptance;
5. evidence that the task is not already claimed or saturated; and
6. expected net value sufficient to justify the build after payout friction, taxes/KYC, and human gates are conservatively accounted for.

Any account creation, claim, outreach, KYC, contract acceptance, payment onboarding, or public submission remains a separate Waterfound gate.

## Mission-state effect

- IssueHunt becomes **PASSIVE / CLOSED_PENDING_REACTIVATION**.
- No product wedge is inferred from this channel.
- No code or prototype is admitted.
- Active work remains commercial discovery on sources with current buyer/problem evidence or credibly claimable, explicitly AI-compatible funded work.
- The privacy P0 producer candidate remains inactive and untouched.
