## Trigger

The selected Next.js review-only opportunity had a technically bounded deliverable and a prepared public proof, but its decisive path to revenue depended on:

- creation and completion of a named freelancer profile;
- interactive human verification and CAPTCHA;
- truthful identity- and experience-specific declarations;
- platform eligibility and Connects;
- identity/KYC, tax and payment onboarding;
- contractual acceptance and ongoing authenticated platform operation.

These are not incidental late-stage gates. They are central dependencies of the acquisition and payment channel. The attempted handoff also showed that Waterfound cannot complete the account flow autonomously and that credential transfer would be inappropriate.

For a mission described as mostly One Machine Side, selecting this channel as the active revenue path was therefore a category error. A bounded audit proof does not make the commercial channel machine-operable.

## Decision

1. Demote the Upwork application path from **ACTIVE** to **PASSIVE / HUMAN-OPTIONAL**.
2. Do not require Waterfound to create or finish an Upwork account, solve CAPTCHA, transfer credentials, complete KYC, purchase Connects, or operate the profile for this mission.
3. Preserve the Next.js audit dossier and public-fixture findings as **SUPPORT_ONLY capability evidence**.
4. Do not submit the prepared proposal unless Waterfound independently supplies an already eligible, authenticated operator and separately chooses to revive this exact opportunity.
5. Do not treat the dormant Upwork gate as a mission-level `HUMAN_GATE`; machine-admissible commercial discovery remains.

No application, contact, spend, account installation, credential transfer, KYC, contract, buyer-specific work or payment occurred.

## Revised ACTIVE admission rule

A commercial path may be **ACTIVE** only when the machine can complete the currently selected learning or revenue transition without routine Waterfound operation and without requiring a new external account, CAPTCHA, identity attestation, KYC, credential handoff, paid proposal token, or private customer access as a central prerequisite.

A path can remain ACTIVE before revenue only if its next commercial predicate is machine-admissible, such as:

- public buyer/problem and substitute research;
- a narrow public or synthetic proof;
- production of a private, reviewable sellable artifact;
- evaluation of an already authorized and machine-operable distribution surface;
- measurement of real inbound demand after separately authorized publication.

Marketplace listings that require a named human account may still be used as buyer and price evidence, but not as the primary execution thesis.

## Selection filter for the next candidate

Reject before build if any is true:

1. first buyer contact requires a new marketplace profile or human identity operation;
2. proposal submission requires CAPTCHA, Connects, KYC or routine manual platform work;
3. delivery requires credentials or private access before public/synthetic differentiation is proven;
4. revenue collection cannot occur through an already authorized machine-operable channel;
5. the business is effectively bespoke human freelancing with Waterfound as the operator.

Prefer candidates where Waterfound can autonomously create the whole value-bearing unit and where the only eventual human gate is a bounded authorization to publish, accept a contract, or onboard payment after demand evidence—not repeated operational labor.
