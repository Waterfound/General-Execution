## Substitution boundary

Automated accessibility tooling can identify and normalize some machine-detectable failures, but it cannot establish comprehensive WCAG conformance. W3C's own preliminary-evaluation guidance states that quick checks cover only a few issues and that a page may appear to pass while retaining significant accessibility barriers.

Source: https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/preliminary/

The buyer requirements place the valuable part of the job in:

- actual keyboard and assistive-technology interaction;
- interpretation of user impact;
- complete page/template coverage;
- issue severity and remediation judgment;
- retesting after fixes;
- professional or regulatory accountability;
- certification where explicitly required.

A synthetic or public-fixture scanner report would prove only the already-commoditized automated layer. It would not answer the buyers' acceptance predicate and could create a misleading impression of compliance.

## Decision

Do not build an accessibility scanner, WCAG dashboard, generic issue normalizer, conformance badge, certification report or automated compliance claim.

Do not bid on the observed projects: both are closed, the work requires human test environments Waterfound has not demonstrated, and one requires a credential Waterfound does not hold.

Preserve automated accessibility checks only as **SUPPORT_ONLY** for software quality when independently useful; never present them as a complete audit or legal/regulatory assurance.

Re-enter only if all are true:

1. a fresh buyer requests a bounded machine-detectable subset rather than comprehensive conformance;
2. acceptance explicitly excludes certification, legal assurance and unavailable manual assistive-technology testing;
3. the target URLs and expected output fields are public before outreach;
4. a private sample can demonstrate a material advantage over established scanners, such as reproducible evidence provenance or regression attribution;
5. competition and price support careful verification;
6. Waterfound separately authorizes any proposal, contact, account action, publication, contract or payment/KYC.

## Kill criteria

Reject before build if any one holds:

- the buyer asks for a complete WCAG, statutory or regulatory compliance conclusion;
- an IAAP or equivalent professional credential is required;
- acceptance requires human screen-reader, keyboard, cognitive or mobile usability judgment not available in the execution envelope;
- automated findings are already sufficient through native or established tools;
- the opportunity is closed or materially crowded;
- customer credentials, private staging access or remediation work are required before a bounded public proof.
