## Decision

Do not build a generic competitor-research scraper, dashboard, monitoring product, spreadsheet generator, or strategic-summary agent.

Do not produce a speculative sample for the closed job: the buyer's named competitors and priorities are private, so an arbitrary fixture would prove only generic research capability and would not answer the acceptance predicate.

Preserve this as evidence that buyers value verifiable source links and access dates. Re-enter only when all are true:

1. a fresh, available buyer requests a substantially identical public-source deliverable;
2. the buyer's target set and acceptance fields are publicly specified before outreach;
3. visible competition is low and the buyer has credible payment history or escrow;
4. the price supports the time required for source verification and bounded human review;
5. a private pre-proposal sample can demonstrate materially better completeness, freshness, contradiction handling, or review effort than ordinary search/spreadsheet work;
6. no customer contact, bid, account creation, payment/KYC, contract, or publication occurs without separate authorization.
