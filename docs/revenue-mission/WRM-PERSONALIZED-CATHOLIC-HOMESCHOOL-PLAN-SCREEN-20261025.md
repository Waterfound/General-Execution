# WRM — Personalized Catholic homeschool plan screen (2026-10-25)

## Decision

**KILL the proposed planner implementation before prototype. Preserve the market signal.**

Catholic homeschool families demonstrably buy and praise well-made planners, but both the static artifact and the proposed personalized multi-child scheduling job are already supplied. A new planner would compete on aesthetics/content volume rather than a verified unresolved outcome.

## Desired outcome tested

> Turn a family's chosen curricula, children, available school days and liturgical preferences into a calm, printable and adaptable Catholic homeschool plan.

This was intentionally framed as a completed outcome, not another empty calendar or generic file checker.

## Buyer delight and category payment

The category shows unusually strong evidence of voluntary, satisfied purchasing:

- Etsy lists 217+ Catholic homeschool planning products, with digital planners commonly priced around US$7–US$30 and physical/premium products up to roughly US$63–US$75:
  https://www.etsy.com/market/catholic_homeschool_planning
- Reviews describe products as “awesome and incredibly functional,” “the best Catholic homeschool planner,” and as replacing months of attempted self-design.
- A dedicated Catholic academic planner is listed at US$62 regular price:
  https://catholichomeschoolplanner.com/
- A printable Catholic homeschool planner sells for US$14.99:
  https://www.elizabethclareblog.com/product/catholic-homeschool-planner-pdf/

This establishes a paid category and genuine delight for third-party products. It does **not** establish demand or willingness to pay for Waterfound.

## Proposed differentiation

The proposed narrow difference from static PDFs was:

- multiple children;
- family-selected curriculum;
- shared and individual lessons;
- available days and weekly capacity;
- liturgical calendar integration;
- printable schedule;
- automatic reflow after missed days;
- no need for continuous Waterfound involvement.

## Direct substitution

The differentiated job is already covered:

1. **Faith Chalk** explicitly combines the Catholic liturgical calendar, Catholic curriculum organization, virtue-based education, multiple children, individualized plans and prayers/devotions:
   https://www.faithchalk.com/features
2. **FaithSchool** supports multiple students, lessons, attendance, hours, grades, reports, AI curriculum generation and BNCC/Common Core mapping:
   https://faithschool.app/
3. **HomeschoolDesk.AI** supports up to ten children, shared and individual courses, curriculum ingestion and automatic scheduling:
   https://homeschooldesk.ai/
4. **Done With School** creates personalized daily checklists for multiple children from enrolled courses and the family calendar:
   https://donewithschool.com/
5. **Hey Juniper** uploads curriculum/pacing guides, extracts lessons, assigns them to multiple students and calculates pacing:
   https://www.juniperhomeschool.com/features
6. **Uschool** turns photographed or uploaded curriculum contents into editable per-child plans:
   https://www.uschool.io/
7. **Kindred** automatically reschedules homeschool and includes Roman Catholic and other liturgical calendars:
   https://plankindred.app/
8. **AGAPAY Learn** demonstrates the same church-rhythm planning model in the Orthodox market, including multiple children and printable plans:
   https://agapay.app/learn

## Evidence classification

- Buyer/problem evidence: credible; organization burden and desire for faith-integrated planning are visible.
- Paid category: confirmed for third-party planners/apps.
- Buyer delight: confirmed for third-party products.
- Waterfound capability: not tested; substitution failed first.
- Competitive differentiation: absent for the proposed feature set.
- Waterfound demand/WTP: unknown.
- Revenue: USD 0.

## Integrity constraints

Any Catholic product would require:

- faithful and source-pinned liturgical/calendar data;
- careful separation of universal, national, diocesan and optional observances;
- no fabricated prayers, obligations, saints, feast ranks or doctrinal claims;
- copyright discipline for modern liturgical texts and commercial curricula;
- clear scope where family judgment or ecclesiastical authority is required.

Those constraints are compatible with the Constitution but raise the evidence threshold. They do not create differentiation by themselves.

## Kill criteria applied

Killed because:

1. exact Catholic multi-child/liturgical planning software exists;
2. curriculum ingestion and adaptive scheduling are common adjacent features;
3. static Catholic planner supply is dense and price competitive;
4. no verified buyer evidence identifies a missing high-value workflow;
5. differentiation would currently depend on content volume, branding or doctrinal trust without demand evidence.

## Re-entry condition

Keep the category eligible only if public buyer evidence exposes a sharply bounded unresolved outcome, such as a specific cross-curriculum reconciliation task or a jurisdiction/calendar mismatch that incumbents repeatedly fail. Re-entry requires:

- at least three independent buyer problem reports;
- absence of an exact existing solution;
- a source-pinned, auditable output;
- no need for ongoing Waterfound content creation or pastoral support;
- a testable willingness-to-try or WTP path.

## Mission consequence

Candidate status: **PAID AND DELIGHTFUL CATEGORY / PROPOSED JOB DIRECTLY SUBSTITUTED / IMPLEMENTATION CLOSED / MARKET SIGNAL PRESERVED**.

No prototype, publication, outreach, account, curriculum ingestion, AI spend, payment, KYC, contract or recurring entitlement was used.
