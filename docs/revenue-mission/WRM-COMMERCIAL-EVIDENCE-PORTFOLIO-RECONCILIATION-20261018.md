## Active–Secondary–Passive discipline

### ACTIVE — available public-payoff discovery

Search only for a **currently available** paid task that satisfies every predicate:

1. explicit monetary reward or buyer-posted budget;
2. credible issuer or buyer history and verifiable payment terms;
3. no active claimant, existing solution, prior allocation, or race that makes expected value poor;
4. public or already-authorized representative input;
5. objective, locally reproducible acceptance test;
6. no credentials, paid infrastructure, physical hardware, private data, outreach, publication, contract, or payment onboarding before private proof;
7. a reward large enough to justify proof effort after contention and collection risk.

This is a commercial channel, not an instrumentation project. Reject at the first failed predicate; do not build speculative implementations.

### SECONDARY — cross-system diagnostic service evidence

Retain accounting data-path and construction evidence work only as buyer/problem signals. Re-enter when a fresh, unallocated scope exposes enough sanitized input to prove one decision-relevant finding before outreach, or when Waterfound separately authorizes a bounded customer-access experiment. Do not infer that such authorization or demand exists now.

### PASSIVE / SUPPORT_ONLY

Preserve the existing Stripe, CI, restore, change-watch, and evidence-binding probes as capability evidence. Do not extend them unless a named buyer acceptance predicate requires it and no cheaper evidence exists.

## Explicit anti-pattern kill

Do not admit another generic read-only audit micro-tool merely because:

- a public repository can supply a fixture;
- a deterministic finding is technically possible;
- an adjacent buyer paid for remediation;
- the output can be formatted as an evidence packet;
- Waterfound already has governance or provenance machinery.

A new build requires both repeated buyer evidence and an unresolved acceptance outcome not already supplied by native tools, specialists, or ordinary services.
