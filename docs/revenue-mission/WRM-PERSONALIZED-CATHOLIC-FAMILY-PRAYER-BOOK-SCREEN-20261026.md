# WRM — Personalized Catholic Family Prayer Book Screen — 2026-10-26

Status: **PAID_DELIGHT_CATEGORY / MORALLY_COMPATIBLE / DIGITAL_LAYER_LOW_PRICE_OR_FREE / PREMIUM_VALUE_HUMAN_CRAFT / NO_BUILD / CLOSED**

Observed Waterfound revenue: **USD 0**

## Narrow job tested

Create a printable Catholic household prayer booklet personalized to one family, using only allowlisted public-domain or independently licensed prayers:

- family surname and chosen patron saints;
- a family Litany of Saints;
- morning, evening and meal prayers;
- Rosary and selected traditional devotions;
- a simple weekly devotional rhythm;
- source attribution and edition/language labels;
- no invented prayer, spiritual direction, sacramental judgment or claim of ecclesiastical approval.

The product was intended to be a one-time, private, self-service PDF requiring no account or recurring Waterfound operation.

## Commercial boundary

The low-touch digital layer has a low price ceiling because buyers can combine free prayer collections, templates and an inexpensive personalized litany. The visible premium is created by:

- original illustration;
- bookbinding and materials;
- physical production and shipping;
- bespoke consultation about family needs;
- human aesthetic judgment.

Those are legitimate paid crafts, but they violate the mission's near-zero recurring human-operation constraint or introduce fulfillment dependencies.

A purely digital Waterfound booklet would therefore compete mainly on arrangement and typography. “Catholic,” “private,” “no account,” “no AI-written prayers,” “one-time” and “source-pinned” are valuable trust attributes, but current evidence does not show that buyers pay a sustainable premium for them.

## Doctrinal and rights boundary

Any future product in this space must fail closed:

- use only verified Catholic prayers from a pinned edition with a clear reuse basis;
- avoid copyrighted modern liturgical translations unless separately licensed;
- never generate sacramental formulas, blessings reserved to clergy, exorcisms, indulgence claims or spiritual diagnoses;
- never imply an imprimatur, nihil obstat, episcopal approval or official Church status;
- never invent saint patronages, feast dates, quotations or promises;
- distinguish household devotion from the Church's public liturgy;
- minimize family names, intentions and sacramental data;
- provide no personalization around hidden sin, illness, fertility, grief or family conflict.

The moral boundary removes the most commoditized “AI writes your prayer” feature, but no evidence shows that this alone creates sufficient WTP.

## Decision

Close before build.

Do not create:

- another family-prayer generator;
- another Litany of Saints printable;
- another fixed prayer collection with a custom cover;
- an AI-written devotional;
- a synthetic personalized booklet;
- a mock checkout or public landing page.

A synthetic fixture would prove template substitution and PDF layout, predicates already answered by existing products.

## Re-entry criteria

Re-enter only if all are true:

1. at least three independent Catholic buyers explicitly request one repeated family-prayer outcome not satisfied by the generator, litany, children's book or handmade-book substitutes;
2. the requested value resides in safe deterministic composition rather than bespoke illustration, printing or consultation;
3. the complete text corpus has a documented reuse basis and a pinned theological source;
4. every personalized choice can be validated mechanically without free-form doctrinal generation;
5. matched evidence supports materially higher completion/use, gift acceptance or willingness to pay than an USD 8 litany plus a free prayer PDF;
6. a lawful unattended acquisition path exists outside repetitive marketplace work;
7. publication, checkout and financial onboarding remain separately authorized gates.

## Kill criteria

Reject if differentiation is only:

- surname or names;
- patron saints;
- custom cover/color;
- family litany;
- daily prayer schedule;
- printable PDF;
- privacy/no account;
- public-domain prayers;
- no-AI positioning.

Reject if the price premium depends on original art, bookbinding, physical fulfillment, pastoral consultation or manual theological review per order.
