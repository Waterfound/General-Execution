## Native and specialist substitution

### Microsoft Excel

Microsoft's Spreadsheet Inquire/Spreadsheet Compare already provides:

- cell-by-cell comparison of values, formulas, named ranges, formatting, and VBA changes;
- workbook analysis reports covering formulas, cells, ranges, warnings, and error results;
- workbook, worksheet, and cell relationship diagrams;
- exportable analysis data.

Sources:

- https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/compare-workbooks-using-spreadsheet-inquire
- https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/analyze-a-workbook-with-spreadsheet-inquire

This directly substitutes generic workbook diff, dependency mapping, formula-error inventory, and structure-warning reports for eligible Excel editions.

### Specialist financial-model audit

Gridlines publicly offers independent financial-model review using cell-by-cell review and shadow modelling. Its public scope includes formula/reference inconsistencies, uncontrolled links, error codes, financial statements, debt metrics, investor returns, and bid pro formas. It also distinguishes a high-level comfort review from a formal audit backed by professional indemnity cover. Published typical fees are USD 16,000–20,000 for a comfort review and USD 32,000–40,000 for a full audit on a roughly 3,000-unique-formula model.

Source: https://www.gridlines.com/financial-model-audit/

The fee is evidence of an established specialist offer, not Waterfound demand or actual customer payment. More importantly, high-value lender/transaction assurance depends on domain expertise, accepted-auditor status, professional judgment, iteration with clients, and liability cover—not merely formula scanning.

## Commercial decision

### Kill as a product wedge

Do not build or sell:

A public fixture could prove that Waterfound can parse formulas or find seeded errors, but that would answer a technical predicate already covered by native tooling while leaving the buyer predicate unanswered.

### Residual hypothesis — hold only

A narrow residual may exist when a real buyer requests an **independent, factual acceptance test** that native Excel analysis does not answer—for example, validating specified outputs against an independently supplied truth set or comparing a model to an explicit contractual calculation specification.

Do not admit even that residual until all are true:

1. an identifiable payer explicitly requests the same objective acceptance job;
2. the spreadsheet and truth/specification fixture are public or explicitly authorized;
3. the acceptance predicate is not answered by Excel Inquire/Spreadsheet Compare or ordinary model-review tools;
4. the output is factual and review-only, with no regulated or professional assurance claim;
5. the opportunity is fresh, available, and not already allocated;
6. a same-input comparison can measure error recall, false positives, review effort, or terminal-report latency;
7. delivery can be machine-prepared with bounded human review and no credentials, outreach, contract, payment onboarding, or private data before proof.
