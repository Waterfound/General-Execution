# WRM Catholic 2027 Digital Planner Screen — 2026-10-26

## Decision

`BUYER_WTP_CONFIRMED / MARKET_SATURATED / STRONG_FREE_BRAZILIAN_SUBSTITUTES / NO_DIFFERENTIATED_JOB / PAYONEER_KYC_GATE / NO_BUILD / CLOSED`

Do not build a generic 2027 Catholic liturgical planner, printable calendar, prayer tracker, or Goodnotes PDF. The category has real demand, but the proposed job is already served by established paid sellers, Brazilian publishers, and free current-year resources.

## Narrow job tested

> Sell a carefully sourced 2027 Catholic liturgical and devotional planner as an automatically delivered digital download, with Portuguese/Brazilian localization and optional English, requiring near-zero routine human operation.

The model was attractive under One Machine constraints because a finished PDF can be delivered automatically and has no inventory or fulfillment.

## Buyer and willingness-to-pay evidence

Etsy shows substantial paid activity in the category:

- the Etsy Catholic planner PDF category exposes 697+ items;
- current 2027 printable calendars are listed around USD 3.50–11.99;
- larger Catholic planner PDFs are listed around USD 7.49–15;
- digital Catholic planners reach about USD 24–28;
- established shops/listings display review histories from dozens to thousands.

Brazilian paid substitutes also exist:

- Editora Santa Cruz sells a 2027 Catholic planner at a displayed price of R$64.90;
- Edições CNBB lists a 2027 monthly liturgical planner at R$14.90;
- multiple current Brazilian physical planners target devotional planning, novenas, and Catholic routine;
- Hotmart contains current and historical Catholic printable planner products.

This confirms category-level WTP. Review counts belong to shops/listings and do not prove unit sales for an individual 2027 SKU.

## Competitive substitution

The generic job is saturated:

- yearly/monthly/weekly Catholic planners;
- USCCB-aligned calendars;
- traditional/1962 calendars;
- homeschool planners;
- Goodnotes/digital planners;
- novena and prayer trackers;
- sacred-art calendars;
- Portuguese generic planners.

Brazil also has strong free substitution. Portal Kairós released free 2027 PDF materials in October 2026, including a monthly planner, portrait and landscape liturgical tables, and book-format calendars. GCatholic exposes a Brazil-specific 2027 calendar.

A localized translation or visual refresh is not sufficient differentiation. A generic devotional tracker would compete with free printables and established sellers on design, reviews, and audience rather than on an unresolved job.

## Channel fit and gate analysis

Etsy supports Brazilian sellers, but official current terms require Etsy Payments through a Payoneer Payment Account for Brazil. Opening a shop also requires identity verification and may require government ID/selfie verification.

Official fee information observed for Brazil:

- Etsy Payments processing: 6.5% + USD 0.30 per order;
- this is additional to Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee;
- Payoneer may impose withdrawal minimums and additional fees.

At low printable prices, fixed and percentage fees materially reduce the already-small gross margin. A new shop would also begin with no reviews or listing history. This is not a reason to reject Etsy categorically; it is a reason not to spend Waterfound's scarce KYC/setup attention on an undifferentiated item.

## Moral and accuracy constraints

A Catholic product must not misstate liturgical dates, rankings, readings, obligations, approved devotions, or local calendars. Calendar differences among the General Roman Calendar, Brazil's proper calendar, diocesan calendars, and 1962 usage must be explicit.

Selling layout, organization, original art, and careful compilation can be morally compatible. Grace, indulgences, sacramental efficacy, or guaranteed spiritual outcomes must never be marketed as commodities. No fake scarcity, manipulative piety, or unverifiable ecclesiastical approval is admissible.

These constraints are manageable, but they increase verification cost and do not create differentiation by themselves.

## Capability versus outcome

### Capability

- Waterfound can produce high-quality Catholic visual and devotional artifacts.
- A PDF/Goodnotes product is technically feasible and automatically deliverable.
- Current liturgical sources and localized calendars are publicly available.

### Observed commercial outcomes

- Category-level paid demand is visible.
- No buyer demand for Waterfound's exact formulation has been observed.
- No unique unresolved job, external willingness-to-try, sale, review, or revenue exists.
- Revenue remains USD 0.

## Kill criteria applied

Close before build because:

1. at least five established paid substitutes cover the same planner job;
2. free current 2027 Brazilian substitutes cover the core informational value;
3. the remaining difference is primarily visual/editorial rather than an unresolved buyer outcome;
4. marketplace entry requires Payoneer, identity verification, fees, and public publication;
5. no evidence supports enough demand to justify that gate.

## Reactivation criteria

Reopen only if public evidence reveals a narrower job that is not served, such as:

- a diocesan/parish operational planner with an authorized buyer and measurable coordination outcome;
- an accessibility need that current planners fail;
- a specific language/community segment with observable search demand and fewer than three direct substitutes;
- a buyer-precommitted custom compilation with clear source authority; or
- a reusable non-devotional planning mechanic that demonstrates willingness-to-pay independently of religious branding.

Any reopening must source dates from authoritative calendars, label calendar scope, and pass a page-level accuracy audit before publication.
