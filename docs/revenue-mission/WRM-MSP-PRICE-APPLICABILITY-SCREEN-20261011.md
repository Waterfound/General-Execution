# WRM commercial screen — MSP price-notice applicability (2026-10-11)

Status: candidate research only. No customer validation, product freeze, pricing, WTP or revenue.

Buyer hypothesis: a small managed-service provider needs to determine which client subscriptions are affected by vendor price or billing changes, when changes apply, and what remains unknown.

First-hand public anecdotes:
- 2026-02-03 MSP discussion describes short-notice vendor increases and difficulty passing costs through to customers: https://www.reddit.com/r/msp/comments/1qv47x8/30_days_price_increase_notices_rant/
- 2026-08-19 MSP discussion reports a renewal charge inconsistent with the displayed plan rate: https://www.reddit.com/r/msp/comments/1vt3e57/freshdesk_renewal_price_increase_with_no_prior/
These are unverified reports, not buyers or WTP.

Official source discriminator:
- Microsoft Partner Center August 2026 notice corrects an earlier effective date to 2026-10-01 for a 5% uplift on annual-term CSP software billed monthly. Existing affected subscriptions change at renewal; annual-billed and month-to-month plans are not affected by that notice. Product examples include Windows Server, SQL Server, CALs and System Center. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-august
- Microsoft Partner Center October 2026 notice corrects Copilot Business usage-based billing default from November 2 to December 1, 2026, for new purchases, initially excluding Brazil. It separately discusses changes in reseller base margin without corresponding customer price changes. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/announcements/2026-october
- Thus a blanket 'all Microsoft 365 plans rise 5%' or 'Copilot usage billing activates in Brazil December 1' claim is unsupported. Exact applicability needs product/SKU, contract term, billing cadence, renewal date, country, and current authoritative price list.

Direct substitutes: Partner Center official notices themselves; Pax8 automatic retail pricing functionality (https://www.pax8.com/blog/microsoft-update-june-2025/); MSPBilling billing reconciliation (https://mspbilling.cloud/); ScalePad lifecycle management (https://www.scalepad.com/lifecycle-manager/pricing); MSPCost contract repricing calculator (https://www.mspcost.com/tools/msp-contract-repricing-calculator). Vendor-described capabilities are not independently benchmarked.

Ratchet decision: KILL generic alerts, reminders, vendor price summaries and margin dashboards as unique claims. HOLD only the narrow evidence-cited cross-notice applicability and supersession exception memo, which is not yet differentiated. The official notices already supply the important distinctions. No product build is justified yet.

Next admissible research: locate a verifiable case of contradictory cross-vendor notices whose applicability is not resolved by ordinary official reading, and compare a minimal action memo with incumbent outputs. If no incremental decision survives, kill this wedge and advance a different buyer/job. No private customer data, contacts, paid accounts, releases, merges, runtime changes or authority changes.
