## Automation compatibility is not established

Algora's general Terms of Service, last marked updated August 17, 2021, do not contain a specific AI-contribution policy. They do, however, prohibit using a robot, spider or other automatic device, process or means to access the service for any purpose, including monitoring or copying its material.

A public bounty issue may separately tolerate AI-assisted code, but that does not override the platform terms or authorize autonomous access to Algora. The terms also require an adult account holder with authority to accept the agreement and accurate account information.

Accordingly:

- disclosed AI assistance in a repository is not the same as permission for unattended machine participation in Algora;
- silence in an issue or maintainer policy is not affirmative authorization;
- any future candidate needs exact rules compatibility before platform interaction.
