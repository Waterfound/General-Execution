## Substitution boundary

The reusable software layer is already mature:

- UK AISI Inspect supplies composable datasets, agents, tools, scorers, sandboxes and custom evaluation tasks.
  Sources: https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/ and https://inspect.aisi.org.uk/scorers.html
- LangSmith runs offline evaluation against curated datasets, custom evaluators and experiment comparisons.
  Source: https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/evaluation-types
- Hugging Face Lighteval evaluates models across multiple backends and stores sample-level results.
  Source: https://github.com/huggingface/lighteval
- Ragas generates synthetic test datasets, including for agent and tool-use workflows.
  Source: https://docs.ragas.io/en/latest/concepts/test_data_generation/agents/

These tools do not replace expert task authorship, professional judgment or inter-annotator calibration. They do remove generic harness construction, dataset plumbing, scoring and result storage as defensible Waterfound differentiation.

Classify LLM evaluation as **current paid human-specialist demand**, not an autonomous product wedge.

Re-enter only if all are true:

1. a fresh unallocated buyer requests a narrow software-engineering or agent-authority benchmark inside Waterfound's demonstrated competence;
2. machine assistance and authorship are expressly allowed and disclosed;
3. the target uses public, synthetic or explicitly authorized inputs;
4. acceptance is deterministic and independently reproducible;
5. the buyer identifies a gap not already covered by Inspect, LangSmith, Lighteval, Ragas or ordinary tests;
6. delivery is a bounded artifact rather than ongoing annotation labor;
7. Waterfound separately authorizes any proposal, outreach, account action, publication, contract or payment/KYC.

## Kill criteria

Reject before proof if any one holds:

- the work requires a professional credential or real-world domain experience Waterfound has not established;
- the buyer prohibits LLM-generated or machine-assisted material;
- acceptance depends on subjective annotation or inter-annotator agreement rather than a deterministic oracle;
- the engagement is already awarded, closed or materially crowded;
- inputs, model outputs or rubrics are private and unavailable before authorization;
- the deliverable is recurring human labor rather than a bounded reusable artifact;
- an established evaluation framework already answers the same technical predicate.
