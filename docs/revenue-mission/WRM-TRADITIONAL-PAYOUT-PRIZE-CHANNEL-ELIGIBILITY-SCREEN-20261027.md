# WRM traditional-payout prize channel eligibility screen — 2026-10-27

## Decision

**Disposition:** `NON_CRYPTO_ONLY / CURRENT_HIGH-VALUE_CONTESTS_INELIGIBLE / PLACEHOLDER_LISTING_REJECTED / TWO_PRE-RULES_WATCHES / NO_BUILD`

Waterfound declined establishing a crypto wallet for commercial discovery. Agent402/x402 and Stacker News therefore remain outside the ACTIVE revenue frontier unless a future route can use an already-authorized non-crypto payer without new wallet setup.

A fresh screen of public AI/software prize competitions found real traditional-currency prize pools, but none is currently a lawful, build-ready Waterfound candidate:

1. PayPal AI Hackathon — Brazil is explicitly ineligible.
2. Qloo Agentic Hackathon — Brazil is explicitly ineligible.
3. UGREEN AI NAS Hackathon — Brazil is explicitly ineligible.
4. Meta VR Start Developer Competition — Brazil is explicitly ineligible and entry additionally requires Meta developer/VR Start access.
5. HackTheFashion Global'26 — the public page still contains unresolved organizer placeholders for the API name, API signup URL, credits, deadline, and prize pool; it is not a reliable executable offer.
6. Google-managed `#BuildHybridAI 2026` — submissions do not open until 2026-11-09 and the public page says complete rules are not yet released. The displayed prize total is internally inconsistent ($5,000 header vs $10,000 prose).
7. Kiro-managed `Kiroween 2026` — submissions do not open until 2026-10-30; the public page explicitly says not to start building yet, prizes/judging are TBD, and complete rules are not yet released.

No prototype, contest account, developer account, entry, publication, contact, wallet, spend, contract, or KYC step was created.

## Commercial predicate

Can a public cash-prize competition provide a near-zero-human, non-crypto route to first revenue for Waterfound, with public inputs and a private proof before registration/publication gates?

**Current answer:** no build-ready candidate was found.

The channel remains potentially useful because it exposes real cash pools and some contests publish enough technical detail for a private prototype. However, legal eligibility and final rules are hard preconditions, not downstream paperwork. A nominal prize cannot be treated as addressable revenue when Brazil is excluded or the organizer has not published operative terms.

## Commercial information gained

1. Traditional-currency prize pools exist, but the highest-value public AI contests sampled are not addressable by a Brazil-resident Waterfound entrant.
2. Legal/geographic eligibility must be checked before product ideation, API setup, or prototype construction.
3. Low participant count is not sufficient opportunity evidence: unfinished organizer templates can create false high-EV signals.
4. A credible future contest must publish final rules, a consistent prize structure, Brazil eligibility, build-period rules, required technology, and payout conditions before it can enter ACTIVE.
5. Contest prizes are not customer WTP for a reusable Waterfound product. Even a suitable contest would validate only judged prize demand unless the job maps to an independent buyer problem.
6. Refusing a crypto wallet does not force a human gate; it removes crypto-pay rails and increases the selection weight of lawful bank/PayPal/cash routes that explicitly admit Brazil.

## Kill and reactivation criteria

### Kill — met for current candidates

Reject without building when:

- Brazil is excluded;
- final rules or prize terms are missing, contradictory, or template placeholders;
- the rules prohibit development before a future start date;
- required accounts, hardware, credentials, or publication arrive before a differentiated private proof;
- the task is judged spectacle without a transferable buyer problem;
- expected value is dominated by extreme competition or unverifiable payout conditions.

### Reactivation

Promote a contest to ACTIVE only when all are true:

1. official final rules are live and source-pinned;
2. a Brazil-resident individual or eligible Waterfound entity may lawfully enter and receive cash;
3. the prize amount and payout path are internally consistent;
4. building has officially opened;
5. the required inputs are public or synthetic and can be used lawfully;
6. a narrow private proof can be built before account/publication/contract gates;
7. the job maps to an independently evidenced buyer problem, not generic agent governance;
8. the expected-value case survives participant count, judging subjectivity, required effort, and opportunity cost;
9. no crypto wallet or crypto payout is required.
