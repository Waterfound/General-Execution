## Mission-local objective

Search for a global, self-serve website that delivers a concrete result without privileged installation and can plausibly support international card payments. Cryptocurrency may be offered later only as an optional rail; it is not a product thesis or prerequisite.

No build is admitted until a wedge has a named buyer/problem, an unresolved competitive gap, a public or user-supplied proof path, and explicit kill criteria.

## Prescreen result

### 1. Semantic third-party API documentation change monitor

Proposed job: given public API documentation or changelog URLs, identify materially relevant changes with before/after evidence when no maintained OpenAPI spec exists.

Result: `REJECT / DIRECTLY_OCCUPIED`.

- BreakWatch already accepts any public docs/changelog URL, requires no installation, classifies changes as breaking/additive/docs-only, provides alerts and history, and charges €29/month: <https://breakwatch.dev/>.
- API Docs Changelog Diff Monitor already turns stored documentation, SDK, changelog, and pricing snapshots into structured endpoint/auth/quota/deprecation signals: <https://apify.com/junipr/api-docs-changelog-diff-monitor>.
- APIDiffGuard covers live response drift with hosted, self-hosted, and CLI modes from US$19/month: <https://apidiffguard.com/>.
- oasdiff covers machine-readable specifications and provides a browser path without installation: <https://www.oasdiff.com/docs/monitor-external-apis>.

No narrower unresolved job or buyer demand specific to Waterfound was found. Do not build.

### 2. Public incident + SLA potential-credit evidence packet

Proposed job: combine public provider incidents and published SLA terms into a bounded packet of possible credit eligibility and missing customer-impact evidence, without claiming legal entitlement.

Result: `REJECT / DIRECTLY_OCCUPIED / PRIVATE-EVIDENCE DEPENDENCY`.

- SLA Credit Watch already exposes incident eligibility checklists and an optional review path: <https://slacreditwatch.com/>.
- UptimeAudit monitors cloud outages, tracks deadlines, generates evidence packages and claim templates, and charges US$5/month for Pro: <https://www.uptimeaudit.io/>.
- Reclivio offers a no-account breach check plus monitoring, contract extraction, credit estimates, and draft requests: <https://reclivio.com/>.
- Complaya sells automated multi-vendor credit recovery to enterprises: <https://complaya.ai/>.
- Microsoft documents that public incident information is insufficient: claims require tenant/outage identifiers and proof that the customer was affected: <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/partner-center/billing/request-credit>.

A public-only Waterfound site could not establish the decisive predicate. Do not build.

### 3. App privacy-label and permission change monitor

Proposed job: source-pinned changes in Apple App Privacy labels and Google Play Data Safety/permissions for compliance and competitive intelligence.

Result: `REJECT / DIRECTLY OCCUPIED`.

- privacybench already maintains a public catalog of 464,201 iOS apps and 197,024 developers, retains historical snapshots, exposes change history, and sells full reports, CSV, alerts, and API access: <https://privacybench.online/>.
- Apify already offers Apple privacy-label snapshots/monitoring and Google Play Data Safety/permissions audits: <https://apify.com/automation-lab/apple-app-privacy-labels-scraper> and <https://apify.com/reaperoak/google-play-data-safety-privacy-audit>.
- Google Checks offers no-integration compliance reports, data monitoring, and change alerts: <https://checks.google.com/pricing/>.
- Similarweb exposes app changelog history including permissions, privacy policy, SDKs, pricing, and store metadata: <https://docs.similarweb.com/api-v5/api-reference/app-analysis-api/app-changelog>.

No defensible residual gap was found. Do not build.

## Commercial information gained

All three jobs have real buyers and existing paid substitutes, which proves category spending but also shows that Waterfound's initial formulation would be a copy without a differentiated result. Existing payment pages also demonstrate that card billing is operationally plausible once a product earns admission; payment setup is not the current bottleneck.

No website, checkout, pricing page, crypto rail, or runtime was built. The binding missing predicate is an unresolved buyer job, not technical capability or payment infrastructure.

## Admission rule for the next wedge

The next global website candidate must pass all six tests before implementation:

1. at least two independent public problem signals from the same buyer segment;
2. a narrow job stated as an input and a verifiable output;
3. a substitute map showing one material result existing products do not provide;
4. delivery from public or user-supplied data without new account permissions;
5. a manual single-use proof that can be evaluated before recurrence or checkout; and
6. kill criteria that close the wedge after at most three representative fixtures.

Paid competitors count as category/WTP evidence only. They do not prove Waterfound demand or authorize copying their product.
