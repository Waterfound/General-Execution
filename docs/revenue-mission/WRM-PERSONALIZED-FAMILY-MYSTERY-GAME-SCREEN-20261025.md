# WRM — Personalized family mystery-game screen (2026-10-25)

## Decision

**KILL before prototype; preserve the category benchmark.**

Personalized print-and-play mystery games satisfy many mission constraints: buyers pay for delight, delivery is digital, marginal fulfillment can be automated, and no continuing Waterfound operation is intrinsically required. However, the exact instant personalized product is already densely supplied at low prices, often with a free first game.

## Outcome tested

> Enter guest names, ages, relationships, occasion, tone and available play time; receive an instantly playable, family-safe mystery with logically sufficient clues, private character sheets, a spoiler-safe host guide and printable/mobile delivery.

The intended distinction from a simple scavenger-hunt generator was a coherent social experience with a uniquely solvable conclusion.

## Evidence classification

- Buyer delight/category payment: credible for third-party products.
- Automation fit: credible at category level.
- Capability: not built; substitution failed first.
- Observed Waterfound outcome: none.
- Competitive differentiation: absent for personalization, speed, format, player count, family suitability and claimed logical QA.
- Waterfound demand/WTP: unknown.
- Revenue: USD 0.

## Quality and moral constraints

A future entertainment product would need:

- age-appropriate content and explicit controls;
- no sexualized, occult-promotional, cruel or degrading content;
- avoidance of inserting real guests into defamatory, humiliating or traumatic roles;
- deterministic consistency checks for culprit, motive, opportunity, clue sufficiency and contradictions;
- preview/refund handling for generation failures;
- honest language: automated checks do not prove that a game is fun or perfectly balanced.

These are product obligations, not differentiation by themselves.

## Kill criteria applied

Killed because:

1. at least five exact instant-personalization competitors exist;
2. entry pricing falls to US$4.99–US$7 and the first game is often free;
3. competitors already claim names, relationships, inside jokes, printable/mobile delivery and logical checking;
4. family-friendly ready-made alternatives are plentiful;
5. no narrow audience or mechanic with verified unmet demand was found.

## Re-entry condition

Reconsider only with evidence for a sharply different mechanic or audience whose constraints competitors do not satisfy, supported by at least three independent buyer reports and a testable willingness-to-try path. “Cleaner,” “more personalized,” “AI generated,” “instant,” “printable,” or “logic checked” are insufficient re-entry claims.

## Mission consequence

Candidate status: **PAID / DELIGHTFUL / AUTONOMY-COMPATIBLE CATEGORY / EXACT PRODUCT COMMODITIZED / CLOSED**.

No prototype, generation spend, publication, outreach, account, payment, KYC, contract or recurring entitlement was used.
