## A. Small-business accounts-payable exception recovery
**Buyer hypothesis:** an AP lead or outsourced bookkeeper wants to find near-duplicate paid invoices, unapplied credits, vendor-name duplicates and credit/reissue exceptions from exports without an ERP migration.

**Observed pain:** A 2026-10-01 public accounting discussion enumerates duplicate invoice number normalization, duplicate vendor master records, missed credits, vendor statement re-entry and changed-amount duplicates. The poster's experience, identity, purchasing authority and magnitude are unverified. Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/AskAccounting/comments/1wuvsn1/duplicate_payments_that_get_past_erp_duplicate/

**Direct first-party substitutes inspected on 2026-10-12:**
- Invogi explicitly offers a no-card, no-integration CSV/XLSX historical AP risk scan, fuzzy duplicate detection, supplier anomalies and evidence-backed findings, with 500 free records: https://invogi.com/product/ap-risk-scan ; https://invogi.com/
- Invoice Auditor markets a free read-only QuickBooks audit including near duplicates, duplicate vendors and unused credits: https://invoiceagent.ignidor.com/
- Reconciles offers browser-side vendor statement vs bills, payment/bank matching, normalization, missing invoices and unapplied credits: https://reconciles.ai/accounts-payable-reconciliation
- Cloudsquid explicitly markets recovery audits spanning invoices, payments, statements and unapplied credits: https://cloudsquid.io/solutions/recovery-audits/
- Xelix advertises proactive duplicate-payment controls and statement/credit recovery: https://xelix.com/solutions/ap-recovery-audit-software-solution/

**Verdict:** KILL_AS_UNIQUE_DIFFERENTIATOR for no-integration export scanning, fuzzy duplicate flags, unused-credit detection and evidence-linked exception lists. Directly marketed products already cover the exact job. Claims on vendor pages are not independently benchmarked. No evidence that WRM can deliver superior recall, lower false positives, better acquisition or less human review. Financial reconciliation also raises sensitive-data handling and accuracy burdens. **Do not build.**

## B. Shopify batch/recall traceability for small merchants
**Buyer hypothesis:** an independent store selling perishable/safety-sensitive goods needs to know affected lots, customer shipments and recall response without an enterprise ERP.

**Observed pain:** A 2026-09-05 Amazon seller publicly reported receiving a defective batch of 11,000 units and asked how to respond; this is a self-reported product-quality incident, not evidence of demand for a lot-tracking product or a Shopify buyer: https://www.reddit.com/r/AmazonFBA/comments/1w8ci0l/supplier_messed_up_11000_unit_order/

**Direct first-party substitutes inspected on 2026-10-12:**
- TraceLot offers Shopify lot/expiry, FEFO, shipment traceability and one-click affected-order/customer recall exports: https://www.tracelot.com/shopify/
- LotTrack advertises Shopify batch traceability, recall cases, customer recall emails and CSV import: https://www.lottrack.dev/
- Telesto advertises supplier-to-customer lot traceability and recall reports: https://www.telesto.app/en/features/food-traceability-and-batch-tracking-software

**Verdict:** KILL_AS_UNIQUE_DIFFERENTIATOR for basic batch tracking, lot alerts, one-click recall lists and no-ERP positioning. Existing specialist offerings are direct substitutes. The anecdote does not establish recurring pain or buyer demand for a new entrant. Compliance/safety mistakes could cause real harm, and no autonomous evidence of safe delivery exists. **Do not build.**
