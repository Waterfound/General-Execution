## Commercial predicate

Can Waterfound sell a near-zero-operation, browser-local reconciliation artifact for two CSV/XLSX files—matched, unmatched, duplicate and conflicting rows plus a source-traceable report—without customer-system access or human data-cleaning judgment?

## Exact substitution test

The named artifact is already available at zero or negligible cost:

- ToolPick compares two CSVs by key locally in the browser.
  https://www.toolpick.dev/free-tools/csv-compare
- RowBench compares Excel/CSV locally and exports added, removed, changed and duplicate rows.
  https://rowbench.clsl.net/en/tools/compare/
- AlcorReconcile performs invoice/payment or order/shipment reconciliation locally and explicitly handles duplicate or empty keys.
  https://alcor-industries.com/reconcile/
- ReconNimble provides free local spreadsheet tools for matched, missing, duplicate and conflicting buckets.
  https://reconnimble.com/tools
- CSV Reconcile offers local comparison with normalization, tolerances, column mapping and downloadable reports; a **US$29 one-time** tier adds large files, XLSX/HTML evidence, hashes, profiles and batch operation.
  https://tools.cosmovexapps.com/csv-reconcile
- useme4 exports a nine-sheet XLSX reconciliation workbook including exact/fuzzy matches, ambiguity, differences, duplicates and errors.
  https://useme4.com/spreadsheet-reconciliation/
- Microsoft Power Query natively merges, combines and transforms files and removes duplicates.
  https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/about-power-query-in-excel
  https://learn.microsoft.com/power-query/combine-files-overview

This is direct functional substitution, not merely adjacent tooling.

## Decision

Do **not** build a generic CSV diff, reconciliation report, deduplication utility, local-first spreadsheet comparator, KPI workbook generator or paid wrapper. The exact deterministic artifact is crowded by free browser-local products, native Excel capability and a low one-time paid ceiling. A Waterfound implementation would add no proved commercial result.

The residual higher-value jobs rely on:

- bespoke matching and KPI definitions;
- PDF extraction and verification;
- ambiguous entity resolution;
- customer-specific data cleanup;
- correction rounds and handover;
- private SharePoint or financial-data access;
- writes to customer workbooks.

Those are human/service or credentialed operations, not a near-zero-operation autonomous product under the current envelope.

## Kill criteria reached

- A free direct substitute produces the same matched/unmatched/duplicate/difference artifact: **reached**.
- Paid self-serve price for a materially richer artifact is too low to support differentiation: **reached** (US$29 one-time observed).
- Higher budgets attach to human extraction, cleanup or private-system writes rather than the machine artifact: **reached**.
- Marketplace competition is high for the bounded deterministic task: **reached** (34 proposals on the US$10–30 job; 30 on the broader US$250–750 job).

## Re-entry criteria
