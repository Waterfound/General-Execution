## Commercial question

Can Waterfound sell a nearly autonomous, read-only pre-contact supplier evidence dossier: three source-cited candidates, verified public facts, explicit unknowns, and a recommended next action, without requiring supplier outreach or migration to a procurement platform?

Observed failure: it cannot responsibly rank the top three or recommend one from this brief. The decisive facts require customer clarification, supplier contact, document verification, and often negotiation. Calling this table a qualified shortlist would overstate the evidence.

## Ratchet decision

**KILL_AS_UNIQUE_DIFFERENTIATOR:** generic public-web supplier discovery, three-name shortlists, source-cited capability matrices, and zero-migration pre-contact dossiers. Matchory already advertises requirement-based, source-backed candidate evaluation; Scoutbee and audited marketplaces cover substantial discovery and verification. The bounded proof did not reach the buyer's requested decision.

**DO_NOT_KILL THE BROADER PAID JOB:** the job postings demonstrate that buyers still hire people for sourcing. The paid value appears concentrated in specification clarification, relationship/local-language access, quotation collection, verification, negotiation, samples, and accountable recommendation—not in producing a public-web longlist alone. That is compatible with a future machine-operated service only after external communication authority and an acquisition path exist; those are not current permissions.

No candidate product is promoted. Do not build another supplier-search interface or add instrumentation.

## Explicit kill/advance criteria for any refinement

Advance only if a later case proves all of the following:

1. a specific buyer segment repeatedly pays for a decision deliverable, not merely names;
2. lawful inputs are available and decisive output quality can be independently scored;
3. Waterfound can produce a materially better decision than Matchory/Scoutbee/marketplace-native search at comparable friction;
4. critical facts do not routinely depend on unverified supplier assertions or physical inspection;
5. authorized external communication can be bounded, auditable, and operated with near-zero Waterfound attention.

Kill/refine when the platform output is equivalent, contact dominates value, recommendation truth cannot be scored, or customer acquisition requires routine Waterfound operation.
