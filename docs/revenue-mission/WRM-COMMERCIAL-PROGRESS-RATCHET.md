# WRM Commercial Progress Ratchet — General Execution candidate

This candidate implements the mission-local selection rule authorized by Waterfound
after WRM-DI-002 localized the first observable commercial-progress divergence at
frontier selection.

It is intentionally small:

- it selects; it never dispatches;
- it creates no authority;
- direct commercial work outranks internal support whenever both are admissible;
- after technical capability proof, support work requires a named missing
  commercial predicate;
- later outreach/payment/publication gates do not mask earlier machine-admissible
  commercial work;
- prerequisites remain fail-closed;
- information gain is the primary ordering within direct commercial frontiers;
- stage maturity breaks ties only after prerequisites are satisfied.

This is not a new System and does not replace Execution Protocol, Work-Sparse,
Total Systems Steward or Durable Execution. The ratchet decides **what economic
frontier deserves attention**; existing systems still decide **how admissible work
is executed**.

No customer outreach, publication, payment, KYC, credentials, paid spend,
persistent-runtime activation, merge or release authority is created.
