## Independent evidence, with limits
The 2026 research study *Analyzing Message-Code Inconsistency in AI Coding Agent-Authored Pull Requests* examined 23,247 agentic PRs and manually annotated 974. It reports 1.7% high message-code inconsistency, with high-MCI PRs associated with lower acceptance (28.3% vs 80.0%) and longer merge time (55.8 vs 16.0 hours). Association is NOT causation; buyer-segment incidence and willingness to pay are unknown. https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04886
A self-reported small team exercise describes three junior reviewers relying on agent-generated PR descriptions rather than inspecting diffs. This is an anecdote, not a validated buyer or audited failure rate. https://www.reddit.com/r/TechLeader/comments/1wu7u7q/our_new_hires_approve_agent_prs_without_reading/
