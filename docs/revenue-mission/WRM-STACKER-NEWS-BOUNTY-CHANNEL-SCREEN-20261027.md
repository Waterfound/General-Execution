# WRM Stacker News bounty channel screen — 2026-10-27

## Decision

**Disposition:** `WATCH_ONLY / CURRENT_CANDIDATES_REJECTED / NO_BUILD`

Stacker News demonstrates real satoshi payouts, but the newly detected 5,000-sat `Show Your Build Weekly #7` item is not an admissible Waterfound product-validation candidate. Its recurring acceptance pattern is a **physical construction/fabrication proof** selected subjectively by community response. Waterfound's present software/agent evidence does not satisfy that job. Preparing a software submission would test neither product value nor the stated contest predicate.

A second detected stock-direction contest is excluded as speculation/gambling-like and therefore fails the mission's moral-compatibility screen.

No outreach, account creation, wallet attachment, public comment, publication, fee payment, or build was performed.

## Commercial predicate

Can a current Stacker News bounty provide a lawful, morally compatible, machine-admissible route to first revenue using existing Waterfound capabilities, with objective acceptance and a private proof possible before any human gate?

**Answer for the observed candidates:** no.

The channel remains a possible discovery source only if a future item is a concrete software/technical job with objective acceptance, a credible real-sats payout, no active claimant, public inputs, and a useful private proof that can be completed before account/wallet/publication steps.

A public third-party radar snapshot in `relayhop/sn-monetization-runtime` recorded Stacker News item `1602563`, titled `Show Your Build Weekly #7: Post Your Proof-of-Work`, for 5,000 sats, with one comment at the sample time on 2026-10-26.

The radar is not authoritative evidence of current availability. Its repository contains many repeated alert issues and the detected issue itself already contains unrelated proposal material. It is useful for discovery, not for claimant, availability, or payment verification.

### Recurring contest rules and observed outcomes

Indexed Stacker News pages from the same `Show Your Build` series consistently describe a physical, real-world proof-of-work contest:

- Week #1: physical construction or hands-on engineering; photo/video proof; attached Lightning wallet.
- Week #2: photo/video of the completed physical work plus technical breakdown; the winning item was a quartz-kitchen build. A workshop software tool received only an honorable mention.
- Week #3: physical project or site setup, technical breakdown, attached Lightning wallet; 5,000 sats paid.
- Week #5: tradespeople, fabricators, and DIY builders; real-world proof-of-work; one entry per participant; 5,000 sats.

Sources:
- https://stacker.news/items/1587083
- https://stacker.news/items/1589476
- https://stacker.news/items/1591593
- https://stacker.news/items/1596748

These recurring rules are strong evidence of the intended job and selection mechanism, but they are not proof that Week #7 remains open or that its wording is identical. Direct current-page verification was blocked by the site's JavaScript/robot surface, so the exact current deadline and current availability remain unverified.

### Channel constraints

Stacker News states that:

- real sats require an attached Lightning wallet;
- users without an attached wallet earn Cowboy Credits, which are not withdrawable as money;
- posting and commenting can incur small fees;
- Rewards require an account and may require personal information or a valid Lightning invoice;
- reward availability, timing, and transfer are not guaranteed until transferred.

Sources:
- https://stacker.news/faq
- https://stacker.news/terms
- https://stacker.news/guide

Those requirements create later account, wallet, publication, and possibly payment gates. They do not justify a gate request here because the currently observed item already fails product fit before those gates.

## Kill and reactivation criteria

### Current candidate kill criteria — met

Close the current item without implementation when any of the following is true:

- the recurring job requires physical construction/fabrication not available from current Waterfound work;
- acceptance is subjective and cannot test the narrow product claim;
- the opportunity is speculative/gambling-like;
- current availability or exclusivity cannot be verified;
- a private proof would not materially improve the chance of acceptance.

### Channel reactivation criteria

Reopen Stacker News as an ACTIVE payoff candidate only when a fresh item has all of:

1. an exact public source and a presently verifiable open state;
2. a credible real-sats amount and payment mechanism;
3. an unclaimed or clearly multi-winner path;
4. a software/technical job matching Waterfound capability;
5. objective, local acceptance tests or a sharply bounded deliverable;
6. a useful private proof achievable before account, wallet, publication, or fee gates;
7. no financial speculation, deception, rights conflict, or prohibited conduct;
8. expected value sufficient to justify the remaining human steps.
